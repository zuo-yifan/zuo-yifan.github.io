About Me

I am currently working as an Associate Professor, Ph.D. Supervisor, and Vice Dean of the School of Computing and Artificial Intelligence at Jiangxi University of Finance and Economics. Focusing on the development trends of high-fidelity and three-dimensional visual information systems, my main academic contributions are in areas such as single-domain image enhancement, cross-domain image enhancement, and multi-view point cloud registration. I have published over 90 academic papers, including more than 30 in ACM/IEEE Transactions journals and Top conferences.

Research Interests

  • Depth Map Enhancement | 深度图增强
  • Image Super-Resolution | 图像超分辨率重建
  • Image Denoising | 图像去噪
  • Depth Estimation | 深度估计
  • Point Cloud Registration | 点云配准
  • AI-Driven Medical Research | AI驱动的医学研究
